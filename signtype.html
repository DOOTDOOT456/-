Letter guide

Hold each sign steady for about half a second to type it — the highlighted key shows what the camera sees right now. An open palm (5) types a space, and J/Z are traced in the air with your index finger. Letters are read by a neural engine (trained on the public ASL Alphabet dataset, so no setup needed), which covers all 24 static letters including N, P and Q. The app also learns from every signer: confident holds are stored in a small in-browser database, grouped per letter, and that learned model helps recognize each person's hand better the more they use it. The shipped copy can come pre-trained (see ✋ Train the shared hand model).

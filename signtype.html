Letter guide

Hold each sign steady for about half a second to type it — the highlighted key shows what the camera sees right now. An open palm (5) types a space, and J/Z are traced in the air with your index finger. Letters are read by a learned engine: a database of real hand samples (a pre-trained seed covers all 24 static letters including N, P and Q) plus a geometric rule classifier, combined by agreement. The app also learns from every signer — confident holds are stored in a small in-browser database, grouped per letter, and recognition gets better the more it's used. The shipped copy comes pre-trained (see ✋ Train the shared hand model).
